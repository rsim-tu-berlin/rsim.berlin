    <a href="https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101296397" target="_blank">PROTEUS</a> is an EU-funded project under the Horizon Europe programme for the period 2026-2030. It pioneers the digital transformation of Copernicus by embedding advanced AI and ML across the full value chain, turning open Earth observation into actionable environmental intelligence. At the centre is the Copernicus Agent, a modular framework that connects multimodal foundation models, hybrid AI–physics simulations, causal reasoning and agentic orchestration into coherent workflows.
